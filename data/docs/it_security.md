# IT Security Policy

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts, including email and the VPN.

All company laptops use full-disk encryption. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the security team within 2 hours of discovery.

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Remote work requires the company VPN at all times when accessing internal systems.
