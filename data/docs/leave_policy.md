# Leave Policy

Every full-time employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 5 days lapses on 31 December.

Sick leave is 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days.

Leave longer than 2 days must be applied for through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance and approved by the reporting manager. Paternity leave is 10 working days; maternity leave is 26 weeks.
