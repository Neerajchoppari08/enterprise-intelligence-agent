# Travel and Expense Policy

Meals during domestic business travel are reimbursed up to Rs 1,500 per day. Receipts are required for any single expense above Rs 500.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims above Rs 10,000 need manager approval; claims above Rs 50,000 also need Finance approval.

Air travel must be booked in economy class through the company travel desk unless the CFO approves an exception.
